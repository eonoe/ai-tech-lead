How it works

Self-guided, but never aimless.

You watch the work done on a real product, then apply the same move to yours. Every phase follows the same loop.

01 LEARN

Concept

A short lesson on the capability, and why it matters.

02 WATCH

Demonstration

See the work on the example project: the prompts, the reviews, and where the agent gets corrected.

03 DO

Apply

Make the same move on your own project, at your own pace.

04 SHIP

Deliverable

Each phase ends in something concrete that ships, plus a short reflection.

Learn at your pace

Everything is recorded and written down, so there's no schedule to keep up with. Pick it up and put it down whenever you want.

Momentum by design

The deep track

Seven phases. Each one installs a capability.

Phases are described by what you'll be able to do, not by a specific app. You apply each to your own project.

The worked example. Across the course, one real product gets built start to finish: an app where the AI does real work inside the product, not a chatbot stapled on the side. Picture something that reads what a user hands it and adapts. You get it as a reference for what good looks like at every phase, and you apply the same moves to your own AI app idea rather than a copy of this one.

Prerequisites

What you should bring

  • Some engineering experience: you already build software in at least one language.
  • Comfort with the command line and basic Git.
  • Access to a coding agent. The free track is the gentlest on-ramp if you're brand new.
